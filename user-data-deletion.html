User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: January 4, 2026 • Last updated: January 4, 2026

One Click Ads AI respects your privacy and your right to control your data. This page explains how you can request deletion of data associated with your use of the app, including data accessed via Meta (Facebook/Instagram).

1. Disconnecting from Meta

You may stop One Click Ads AI from accessing your Meta data at any time by:

  1. Logging into your Meta (Facebook) account
  2. Going to Settings → Business Integrations (or Settings → Apps and Websites, depending on your account)
  3. Selecting One Click Ads AI
  4. Clicking Remove

Once removed, we will no longer be able to make new Meta API requests on your behalf.

2. Requesting Data Deletion

To request deletion of data stored by One Click Ads AI:

  • Email us at ai.oneclick.ads.us@gmail.com
  • Use the subject line: “User Data Deletion Request – One Click Ads AI”
  • Send the request from the email address associated with your One Click Ads AI account

3. What Data Is Deleted

Upon a verified request, we will delete or anonymize, as applicable:

  • Your One Click Ads AI account information
  • Stored Meta ad account identifiers and authorization tokens
  • Cached reporting/analytics data (retained until account deletion unless you request earlier deletion)

Data required for legal compliance, security, or fraud prevention may be retained as permitted by law.

4. Processing Time

We typically process deletion requests within 30 days.
