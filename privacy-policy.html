2) Who this app is for

One Click Ads AI is designed for individual advertisers to create, manage, and analyze their own ad campaigns. You control which Meta ad account(s) you connect and what actions you authorize.

3) What this policy covers

This policy covers information processed through:

  • Our web app and services at Azeem.ca
  • Our integration with Meta (Facebook/Instagram) via the Marketing API
  • Our support channels (email, chat, tickets)

Meta also processes information under its own policies when you use Meta products and services.

4) Information we collect

A. Information you provide to us

  • Account information (name, email, optional profile details)
  • Support content (messages and attachments you send us)
  • Billing metadata (if applicable) handled by our payment processor (we typically receive limited billing information)

B. Information we access from Meta via the Marketing API

If you connect your Meta account and grant permissions, we may access and process data necessary to provide the app’s features, such as:

  • Ad account and asset identifiers (e.g., ad account ID, business/asset IDs)
  • Campaign, ad set, and ad details (names, objectives, budgets, schedules, creative metadata)
  • Performance and reporting metrics (e.g., impressions, clicks, spend, conversions, ROAS)
  • Account-level settings/metadata you have access to (where permitted)
  • Authorization credentials (e.g., access tokens) used to make API requests on your behalf

We do not receive your Meta password. Authorization is handled through Meta’s login/permission flow (OAuth).

C. Automatically collected data

  • Device and usage data (IP address, browser type, pages/events, timestamps)
  • Log data for security and debugging (request IDs, error logs)
  • Cookies or similar technologies (see Section 10)

5) How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate One Click Ads AI (connect to ad accounts, create/modify campaigns where you authorize, fetch reporting)
  • Authenticate you and maintain account/session security
  • Generate dashboards, recommendations, exports, and analytics you request
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Monitor, prevent, and detect fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms
  • Improve the product (reliability, performance, and user experience)

6) How we share information

We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers (processors) that help us run the app (hosting, databases, analytics, customer support, email delivery, payment processing). They may process information only to provide services to us and must protect it.
  • Meta, as needed to use the Marketing API and return results for actions you initiate in the app.
  • Legal/safety, when we believe disclosure is necessary to comply with law, protect rights/safety, investigate fraud, or respond to lawful requests.
  • Business transfers (e.g., merger or acquisition), where information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share data obtained from Meta beyond what is needed to provide the service, except as described above.

7) Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate business purposes (such as security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance). Typical retention periods:

  • Account data: for as long as your account remains active
  • Meta API tokens: stored only as needed to keep the integration working; revoked/expired tokens are removed or rendered unusable
  • Logs: typically retained for 1 year
  • Reporting/analytics caches: typically retained for 7 years (or shorter if you request deletion)

8) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, such as:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and, where appropriate, encryption at rest
  • Access controls and least-privilege permissions
  • Monitoring and logging to detect abuse
  • Secure handling of API credentials and tokens

No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and reduce risk.

9) Your choices and controls (Meta connection)

You can disconnect One Click Ads AI from Meta at any time by removing the app from your Meta account settings. After removal, we will stop making new Meta API calls on your behalf.

10) Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies/local storage and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in and maintain sessions
  • Remember preferences
  • Measure performance and reliability
  • Understand feature usage and improve the product

If you are in a region that requires consent for certain cookies, we will provide a consent mechanism. You can also manage cookies via your browser settings.

11) International data transfers

We may process information in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (such as contractual protections) for cross-border transfers.

12) Data deletion and your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

How to request deletion of your data

You can request deletion of data associated with your use of One Click Ads AI in any of the following ways:

  1. In-app deletion (if enabled): Go to Settings → Privacy → Delete Data and follow the prompts.
  2. Email us: Send a request to ai.oneclick.ads.us@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.
  3. Disconnect from Meta: Remove One Click Ads AI from your Meta account settings to stop future access. If you also want us to delete stored data, email us using option (2).

Once we receive a verified deletion request, we will delete or de-identify information that is not required for legal compliance, security, or dispute resolution.

Meta requires apps to provide a publicly available privacy policy and explain how users can request deletion of their data.
